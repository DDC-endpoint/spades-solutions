## Stack

Plain static HTML / CSS / JS, no build step. Three pages: `index.html` (Home), `services.html` (Services), `contact.html` (Contact / Quote). Served locally with `npx --yes serve . -l 3002`. Static-host deploy (target host not yet chosen).

## Users

General audience across Colombo and surrounds; no single segment leads the copy:

- Schools: event committees and teachers organising concerts, prize givings and sports meets; class, club and house T-shirts.
- Companies: launches, conferences and staff events; staff uniforms and branded merchandise.
- Weddings and private functions.
- Concerts and festivals.
- Sports teams and clubs: kits.

Typical visitor arrives from Instagram or word of mouth, wants to know quickly whether Spades can handle their event or order, and then asks for a quote.

## Product Purpose

Spades Solutions runs two independent services:

1. **Custom Apparel & Printing** — custom T-shirts and apparel, bulk printing, embroidery, branded merchandise, design support.
2. **Event Logistics** — the physical production of an event: sound and lighting (AV), staging and structures (stages, trussing, tents, LED screens, backdrops), transport and setup/teardown crew, and planning and on-site coordination.

"Logistics" always means **event logistics**. It never means freight, courier, or garment delivery.

Success for the site: a visitor understands which service they need and sends a quote request (form or WhatsApp).

## Positioning

The two services are presented **separately**, not as a combined "one vendor for everything" pitch. Each must stand on its own and gets its own quote. Visitors can book either one, or both.

## Product Principles

1. Make it obvious within seconds that there are two distinct services and which one fits the visitor.
2. Speak to every audience equally: schools, companies, couples, promoters and teams.
