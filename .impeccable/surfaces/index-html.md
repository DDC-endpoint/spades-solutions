## Direction contract

THESIS: The logo is a spade, so each service is dealt as its own playing card. Refuses the category default of a dark concert photo hero over a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy-black table (#0A1628), navy card faces (#12284A / #1B355E), silver (#CDD5E0) for ink accents, buttons and frames; no third hue. Cards and panels carry the badge logo's double ring as a two-hairline inner frame. Marcellus (Roman capitals, echoes the wordmark) for headings and card ranks; Figtree for body. Card corner indices: rank + logo spade pip, mirrored bottom-right.

STORY: The visitor sees two separate services at once, understands what each covers, and takes the quote action printed on the card for the one they need.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (spade + wordmark, nav, silver Get a Quote pill). Centred Marcellus headline and one-line lede. Below, a fanned hand of three cards: Apparel & Printing face (A) left, the filigree card back centre, Event Logistics face (K) right; each face lists four inclusions and its own quote button.

Signature interaction: the two face cards deal out from behind the card back on load; hover or focus lifts one card at a time. Below 1080px a small card back sits above the two faces and they deal out from it. Reduced motion shows the hand static.
